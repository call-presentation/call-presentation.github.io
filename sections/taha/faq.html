Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the Báb, his claims, and how they relate to Islamic teachings on Imam al-Mahdi (عج).

Who was the Báb?

The Báb, Sayyid Ali Muhammad Shirazi, was a 19th-century figure from Shiraz, Iran who first claimed to be a gate to Imam al-Mahdi (عج) and later escalated his claims.

Who is Imam al-Mahdi (عج)?

Imam al-Mahdi (عج) is the awaited leader in Shia Islam, divinely appointed to establish global justice and peace.

Why is consistency important in divine claims?

Divine authority requires clarity, consistency, and divine designation. Changing claims undermines credibility and contradicts Islamic teachings.

Did the Báb retract his claims?

According to documented accounts, including Baha'i sources, the Báb repented and withdrew his claims when examined by Iranian authorities.
