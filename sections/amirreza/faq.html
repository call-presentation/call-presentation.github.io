Frequently Asked Questions

Clarifying Divine Guidance

Why do sincere people reach opposite conclusions from the same Qur'an?

Because being sincere isn't enough to remove confusion. Without a trusted guide who can explain the text with authority, people will keep reaching different conclusions — even when they all mean well.

What happens when interpretation loses authority?

Religion fragments. Certainty gives way to contradiction, and guidance becomes opinion-based rather than truth-based.

Who establishes the true meaning of Allah's signs?

According to Shia belief, the Imams of Ahl al-Bayt — and ultimately Imam Mahdi (عج) — establish meaning through inherited divine knowledge, not personal reasoning.
