Introduction

اَلا اِنَّهُ الْمُخْبِرُ عَنْ رَبِّهِ وَ الْمُشَیِّدُ لِأَمْرِ آیاتِهِ
"Indeed, he conveys from his Lord and establishes the affair of His signs."

Let me ask something important:

Who explains God's message when interpretations conflict?
Books exist.
Translations exist.
Opinions exist.
But clarity?

This sentence answers that question.

Arabic & Translation

اَلا اِنَّهُ الْمُخْبِرُ عَنْ رَبِّهِ -عَزَّ وَجَلَّ- وَ الْمُشَیِّدُ لِأَمْرِ آیاتِهِ
Translation:
Behold! He conveys from his Lord, the Almighty, and firmly establishes the cause of His signs.

Qur'anic Foundation

Allah says:

﴿وَمَا كَانَ لِبَشَرٍ أَن يُكَلِّمَهُ اللَّهُ إِلَّا وَحْيًا﴾

(Qur'an 42:51)

After the Prophet ﷺ:

  • Revelation ends
  • Explanation and application continue

Another verse:

﴿فَاسْأَلُوا أَهْلَ الذِّكْرِ إِن كُنتُمْ لَا تَعْلَمُونَ﴾

(Qur'an 16:43)

Shia Tafsīr

Ahl al-Dhikr = the Imams of Ahl al-Bayt

وَجَعَلْنَاهُمْ أَئِمَّةً يَهْدُونَ بِأَمْرِنَا وَأَوْحَيْنَا إِلَيْهِمْ فِعْلَ الْخَيْرَاتِ وَإِقَامَ الصَّلَاةِ وَإِيتَاءَ الزَّكَاةِ ۖ وَكَانُوا لَنَا عَابِدِينَ
We made them imams, guiding by Our command, and We revealed to them the performance of good deeds, the maintenance of prayers, and the giving of zakat, and they used to worship Us.

(Qur'an 21:73)

Story from an Imam

People once debated Qur'anic meanings before Imam al-Bāqir (ع). He did not argue emotionally. He explained the context, reason for revelation, and application — and confusion vanished.

Lesson

The Mahdi establishes Allah's signs by:

  • removing contradiction
  • restoring coherence
  • aligning law, ethics, and spirituality

Takeaway

Imam Mahdi (عج) does not bring a new message. He brings:

  • the final clarity
  • the final alignment
  • the lived meaning of Allah's signs

When he speaks, confusion ends — not because people are forced to listen, but because truth finally sounds like truth.
