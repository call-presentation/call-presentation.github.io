Introduction

Would Allah ever leave humanity without guidance? The Qur'an clearly tells us that He would not.

"For every people there is a guide."

(Qur'an 13:7)

This shows that guidance is not just historical—it is always needed.

The Declaration

In the Sermon of Ghadir, the Prophet declares about the final Imam:

اَلا اِنَّهُ الْباقِي حُجَّةً وَلا حُجَّةَ بَعْدَهُ
"Indeed, he is the remaining Proof—and there is no proof after him."

[1]

This statement is definitive. It establishes both the continuation and the conclusion of divine proof on earth.

↑ Back to top

The Concept of Hujjah

To understand this, we must first know what a divine proof, or hujjah, means.

Imam al-Ṣādiq (ع) teaches us a universal rule:

"The earth can never remain without a Hujjah from Allah."

[2]

A hujjah is not just a teacher or scholar. It is a divinely appointed authority whose very existence serves as Allah's argument against ignorance.

↑ Back to top

Why Guidance is Necessary

But why is that hujjah needed?

The Qur'an explains in Surah An-Nisa:

Divine guides are sent "so that mankind will have no argument against Allah after the messengers."

[3]

This creates a theological necessity: a link between God and mankind that removes any excuse for ignorance.

  • Without a living proof, humanity could claim they were not warned
  • Without guidance, people could say they didn't know the right path
  • Without a hujjah, Allah's argument would be incomplete
↑ Back to top

Finality of the Proof

However, the Prophet's declaration adds a critical constraint: Finality.

By calling him the "Remaining Proof," the Prophet defines the Mahdi as the culmination of divine guidance — where the necessity of guidance meets the completion of its purpose.

So, I ask you: If Imam Mahdi is the final proof, what does that mean for anyone claiming divine leadership after him?

Implications

Any such claim is structurally impossible.

This declaration closes the door on future innovation in divine authority. It establishes that:

  • The roadmap is complete
  • The argument is finished
  • No further guides are required to supersede him

Just as Muhammad (ﷺ) is the Seal of the Prophets,
al-Mahdi (عج) is the Remaining Proof—
the final and complete argument of Allah on earth.
