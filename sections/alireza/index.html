Opening

اَلا اِنَّهُ الْمُدْرِكُ بِكُلِّ ثَارٍ لِأَوْلِيَاءِ اللَّهِ
"Indeed, he will attain every blood-right of the friends of Allah."

Let me ask a painful question:

What happens to injustice that history never corrected?
What about:

  • Karbala?
  • Poisoned Imams?
  • Silenced believers?

Does their blood disappear?

This sentence says: No.

Arabic & Translation

اَلا اِنَّهُ الْمُدْرِکُ بِکُلِّ ثَارٍ لِأَوْلِيَاءِ اللَّهِ
Translation:
Behold! He will settle every blood-right of the friends of Allah.

This is not limited to one event. It includes:

  • Karbala
  • Every oppressed believer

Qur'anic Foundation

Allah says:

﴿وَلَا تَحْسَبَنَّ الَّذِينَ قُتِلُوا فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ أَمْوَاتًا﴾
"Do not think those killed in the way of Allah are dead."

(Qur'an 3:169)

And:

﴿وَمَن قُتِلَ مَظْلُومًا فَقَدْ جَعَلْنَا لِوَلِيِّهِ سُلْطَانًا﴾
"Whoever is killed unjustly, We have given authority to his guardian."

(Qur'an 17:33)

Shia Tafsīr

That ultimate wali (guardian) is Imam al-Mahdi (عج).

And:

وَعَدَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ مِنكُمْ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَـٰتِ لَيَسْتَخْلِفَنَّهُمْ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ …
"Allah has promised those among you who believe and do righteous deeds that He will surely grant them succession upon the earth…"

(Qur'an 24:55)

Allah SWT promises a day when justice is fully realized on earth. This is Imam Mahdi's (عج) mission.
