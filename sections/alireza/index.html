بِسْمِ ٱللَّٰهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ اَلا اِنَّهُ الْمُدْرِكُ بِكُلِّ ثَارٍ لِأَوْلِيَاءِ اللَّهِ “Indeed, he will attain every blood-right of the friends of Allah.” Opening (Emotional Hook ) Let me ask a painful question: What happens to injustice that history never corrected? What about: Karbala? Poisoned Imams? Silenced believers? Does their blood disappear? This sentence says: No. Arabic & Translation اَلا اِنَّهُ الْمُدْرِكُ بِكُلِّ ثَارٍ لِأَوْلِيَاءِ اللَّهِ Translation: Behold! He will reach and settle every blood-right of the friends of Allah. This is not limited to one event. It includes: Karbala Every oppressed believer Qur’anic Foundation Allah says: ﴿وَلَا تَحْسَبَنَّ الَّذِينَ قُتِلُوا فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ أَمْوَاتًا﴾ (Qur’an 3:169) “Do not think those killed in the way of Allah are dead.” And: ﴿وَمَن قُتِلَ مَظْلُومًا فَقَدْ جَعَلْنَا لِوَلِيِّهِ سُلْطَانًا﴾ (Qur’an 17:33) “Whoever is killed unjustly, We have given authority to his guardian.” 📌 Shia tafsir explains: That ultimate wali is Imam al-Mahdi (عج). And: Qur’an — Surah An-Nur (24:55) وَعَدَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ مِنكُمْ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَـٰتِ لَيَسْتَخْلِفَنَّهُمْ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ … “Allah has promised those among you who believe and do righteous deeds that He will surely grant them succession upon the earth…” 📌 Allah SWT promiss that there will be a day on earth when justice is completely fulfilled.(mission of imam Mahdi AG) Shia Narration Imam al-Ṣādiq (عليه السلام) says: “The Mahdi will rise with the shirt of Husayn stained in blood.” 📚 Bihār al-Anwār, vol. 52 This is symbolic: Karbala is not closed The case is still open The final verdict has not yet been delivered Story from the Imams Imam al-Riḍā (عليه السلام) said: “Every Imam grieved for Husayn, and the grief continues until the Mahdi rises.” 📚 Kāmil al-Ziyārāt 📌 Lesson: The mission of Karbala is unfinished—and the Mahdi completes it.
