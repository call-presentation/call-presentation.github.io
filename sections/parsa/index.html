Opening Supplication

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
اللهم صلّ على محمد وآل محمد وعجل فرجهم ولعنة الله على أعدائهم أجمعين
O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad and the Family of Muhammad, and hasten their relief, and may Allah's curse be upon all their enemies.

The Seal of the Imams

اَلا اِنَّ خاتَمَ الْاَئِمَّةِ مِنَّا الْقائِمَ الْمَهْدِىَّ
"Verily, the Seal of the Imāms from among us is al-Qā'im al-Mahdī."

Just as Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) is the Seal of the Prophets, Imam al-Mahdi (عج) is the Seal of the Imams. He completes the divine succession of twelve Imams that began with Imam Ali (ع).

The Twelve Imams

The twelve divinely appointed leaders from the family of the Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) are:

1st Imam Ali (ع)
2nd Imam Hasan (ع)
3rd Imam Husayn (ع)
4th Imam Zayn al-Abidin (ع)
5th Imam al-Baqir (ع)
6th Imam al-Sadiq (ع)
7th Imam al-Kadhim (ع)
8th Imam al-Ridha (ع)
9th Imam al-Jawad (ع)
10th Imam al-Hadi (ع)
11th Imam al-Askari (ع)
12th Imam al-Mahdi (عج)

Prophetic Foretelling of Twelve Leaders

Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) foretold twelve successors after him, all from his family. This hadith is narrated in both Sunni and Shia sources:

Narrated in Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim:

"This religion will continue until there are twelve successors over you, all of them from Quraysh."

Shia sources identify these twelve as the Ahl al-Bayt (the Prophet's family), beginning with Imam Ali (ع) and concluding with Imam al-Mahdi (عج).

  • Sahih al-Bukhari, Book 93 (al-Ahkam), Hadith 7222
  • Sahih Muslim, Kitab al-Imarah, Hadith 1821
  • Uṣūl al-Kāfī, Kitāb al-Ḥujjah

Conclusion

The narrations consistently mention twelve divinely appointed leaders from the family of the Prophet ﷺ. The first is Imam Ali (ع), and the final is al-Qa'im al-Mahdi (عج), the Seal of the Imams.

Just as prophethood was sealed with Muhammad (ﷺ),
Imamate is sealed with al-Mahdi (عج).
