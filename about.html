About This Project

Why we created this project, how we approach the topic, and what we hope to achieve.

Purpose of the Study

This project is a collection of presentations about Imam Mahdi (عج) — the twelfth Imam and the Awaited Savior in Shia Islam.

Each presentation looks at the topic from a different angle: evidence from the Quran and hadith, comparing beliefs across Judaism, Christianity and Islam, theological reasoning, and examining opposing claims.

Our goal is to provide well-researched material that anyone can understand, whether you're new to this topic or already familiar with it.

📖

Multi-Topic Research

Twelve separate presentations exploring Imam Mahdi from every important angle.

Methodology

The study uses the Quran, authentic hadith collections (both Sunni and Shia), and respected Islamic scholarship as its main sources. We also reference the Bible when comparing different religious beliefs.

Each topic stands on its own. You can read any presentation that interests you without having to read the others first.

We base everything on evidence, citing our sources and being honest about what we present.

📜

Source-Based Analysis

Based on the Quran, hadith collections, and Islamic scholarship with full references.

Topics Covered

The project covers twelve connected topics:

  • The promised savior in Judaism, Christianity, and Islam
  • Hadith evidence for the twelve chosen successors
  • Imam Mahdi as God's final proof (Hujjah)
  • Why we need divine guidance and a living Imam
  • Justice and retribution in Imam Mahdi's mission
  • How to identify the true Mahdi from false claimants
  • Testing the Báb's claims against Islamic teachings
  • Problems with New Testament end-times texts
  • Divine authority and delegation
  • The conqueror of fortresses and tyrannical systems
  • The guardian of divine trust
  • Allah's chosen one and divine leadership
