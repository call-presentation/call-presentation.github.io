About This Project

Understanding the purpose, approach, and vision behind this multi-topic study of Imam Mahdi and the doctrine of the Awaited Savior.

Purpose of the Study

This project presents a collection of independent presentations exploring the doctrine of Imam Mahdi (عج) — the twelfth Imam and the Awaited Savior in Shia Islam.

Each presentation approaches the topic from a distinct angle: scriptural evidence from the Quran and hadith, comparative analysis across Abrahamic traditions, theological reasoning, and critical evaluation of contrary claims.

The goal is to provide well-researched, accessible material for anyone seeking to understand this fundamental Islamic belief.

📖

Multi-Topic Research

Seven independent presentations exploring the doctrine of Imam Mahdi from every meaningful angle.

Methodology

The study draws primarily from the Quran, authentic hadith collections (both Sunni and Shia), and established theological scholarship. Biblical sources are referenced where comparative analysis is relevant.

Each topic is independently researched and presented, allowing readers to explore any subject that interests them without requiring prior reading.

The approach is evidence-based and scholarly, presenting claims with their supporting sources and maintaining intellectual honesty throughout.

📜

Source-Based Analysis

Grounded in primary scriptural and hadith sources with proper references and contextual analysis.

Topics Covered

The project spans seven interconnected areas of study:

  • The messianic promise across Judaism, Christianity, and Islam
  • Critical examination of the Báb's claims against Islamic criteria
  • Eschatological critique of New Testament texts
  • The necessity of divine guidance and a living Imam
  • Justice and retribution in Imam Mahdi's mission
  • Hadith evidence for the twelve divinely appointed successors
  • Imam Mahdi as the remaining Hujjah (proof) of God
🕌

Comprehensive Scope

From Quranic foundations and prophetic hadith to comparative religion and critical analysis.
